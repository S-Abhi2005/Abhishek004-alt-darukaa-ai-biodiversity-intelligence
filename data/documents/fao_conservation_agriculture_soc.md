# FAO Conservation Agriculture and Soil Organic Carbon (SOC)

## Core Principles of Conservation Agriculture (CA)
Conservation Agriculture is an agro-ecological approach characterized by three linked principles:
1. Continuous minimum mechanical soil disturbance (zero tillage or direct seeding).
2. Permanent soil organic cover with crop residues and cover crops (minimum 30% ground cover).
3. Diversification of crop species grown in sequence and/or association (crop rotations including legumes).

## Soil Organic Carbon (SOC) Accumulation Dynamics
- **Baseline Depletion**: Conventional intensive tillage accelerates microbial oxidation of soil organic matter, leading to typical SOC losses of 30-50% over decades of cultivation.
- **Sequestration Rates**: Full CA systems sequester an average of 0.33 to 0.58 tonnes of carbon per hectare per year (t C/ha/yr) in topsoil (0-30 cm depth). In sub-humid to humid agro-ecozones with continuous cover crops, rates reach up to 0.85 t C/ha/yr.
- **Biochemical Mechanism**: Minimizing soil disturbance preserves stable macro-aggregates (>250 μm). Within undisturbed macro-aggregates, occluded particulate organic matter (iPOM) is protected from microbial enzymes, transforming labile organic inputs into mineral-associated organic matter (MAOM), which possesses a decadal-to-centurial residence time.

## Moisture Retention and Water Infiltration
- Surface residue mulch dramatically reduces solar irradiance at the soil surface, suppressing non-productive evaporation by 25-40 mm per cropping cycle.
- Macropore continuity formed by decaying taproots and earthworm biopores remains intact, boosting saturated hydraulic conductivity ($K_{sat}$) by 2.2x to 4.5x relative to conventionally tilled soils.

## Thresholds and Implementation Rules
- **Minimum Residue Retention**: At least 2.5 tonnes dry matter/ha residue is required to prevent surface crusting and seal formation during high-intensity storm events.
- **Nitrogen Immobilization Risk**: Early transition years (Years 1-3) often exhibit temporary N immobilization due to high C:N ratio crop residues (>40:1). Integration of leguminous cover crops (*Vicia villosa*, *Mucuna pruriens*, *Crotalaria juncea*) is mandatory to maintain available mineral N ($NH_4^+$, $NO_3^-$).
