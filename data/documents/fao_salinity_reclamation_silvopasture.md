# FAO Salinity Reclamation & Silvopastoral Systems

## Diagnosis and Classification of Saline & Sodic Soils
- **Saline Soil**: Electrical Conductivity of saturated extract ($EC_e$) > 4.0 dS/m, Exchangeable Sodium Percentage (ESP) < 15%, pH < 8.5.
- **Sodic (Alkali) Soil**: $EC_e$ < 4.0 dS/m, ESP > 15%, pH > 8.5. High dispersed sodium ions destroy soil aggregates, causing clay swelling and complete loss of infiltration.
- **Saline-Sodic Soil**: $EC_e$ > 4.0 dS/m, ESP > 15%, variable pH. Highly delicate balance; reducing salinity without displacing sodium triggers structural collapse.

## Halophytic Agroforestry & Biodrainage
- **Deep-Rooted Trees as Biological Pumps**: Species such as *Casuarina equisetifolia*, *Acacia nilotica*, *Prosopis juliflora*, and *Eucalyptus camaldulensis* transpire saline groundwater (1,200-2,000 mm/year), depressing shallow saline water tables below the critical root zone (1.5-2.0m depth).
- **Salt Sequestration**: Halophytic fodder shrubs (*Atriplex nummularia*, *Atriplex halimus*) accumulate NaCl in foliar trichomes (10-25% dry weight ash content), extracting up to 1.2 tonnes of soluble salts per hectare per year when harvested for animal fodder.

## Chemical Amendment Protocols
- **Gypsum ($CaSO_4 \cdot 2H_2O$) Requirement**: For sodic soils, divalent $Ca^{2+}$ ions must replace monovalent $Na^+$ on the soil colloidal exchange complex before flushing:
  $$\text{Gypsum Requirement (t/ha)} = \frac{(\text{Initial ESP} - \text{Target ESP})}{100} \times \text{CEC} \times 0.086 \times \text{Bulk Density} \times \text{Depth (m)}$$
- **Leaching Fraction ($LF$)**: Sufficient low-salinity irrigation must pass through root profile to drain displaced salts: $LF = EC_{iw} / (5 \times EC_e - EC_{iw})$.

## Silvopasture Integration
- Grazing halotolerant perennial grasses (*Chloris gayana* - Rhodes grass, *Leptochloa fusca* - Kallar grass) combined with leguminous tree legumes (*Leucaena leucocephala*) restores nitrogen cycling and biological soil structure within 24-36 months.
